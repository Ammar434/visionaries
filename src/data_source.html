Data Sources

Primary Dataset

  • BAAC (Bulletin d'Analyse des Accidents Corporels)
    • Source: French Ministry of Interior
    • Coverage: 2005-2023
    • Description: Official French traffic accident database with detailed information about all reported accidents, including location, severity, and circumstances
    • Format: CSV files with separate tables for characteristics, locations, vehicles, and casualties
    • Update Frequency: Annual
    • Access: data.gouv.fr
