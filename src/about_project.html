About the Project

We tried to examines the correlation between cycling infrastructure development and bicycle accident rates across France from 2005 to 2023.

Research Question

To what extent does the development of cycling infrastructure help reduce the number and severity of cycling accidents?

Project Objectives

  • Analyze 19 years of bicycle accident data across France (2005-2023)
  • Identify patterns in accident frequency, severity, and contributing factors
  • Evaluate the impact of cycling infrastructure development on safety
  • Provide insights for urban planners and policymakers to improve cycling safety

Project Evolution

Initially focused on bicycle sharing services like Vélib in Paris, our research scope expanded to include all cyclists and infrastructure types across French regions to provide more comprehensive insights.
